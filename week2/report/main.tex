\subsection{Critical velocity gradient}
For $\mathrm{Re}_L=10^5$, the critical velocity gradient for turbulent separation to occur at $x/L=1$ is $\dd{(u_e/U)}/\dd{(x/L)}=-0.38$ (no turbulent separation occurs at this value, with turbulent separation occurring at $x/L = 0.975$ for $\dd{(u_e/U)}/\dd{(x/L)}=-0.39$).
